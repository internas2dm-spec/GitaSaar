**App name:** Gita Saar - Bhagavad Gita

Gita Saar is a reading app for the Bhagavad Gita, with daily verse notifications, bookmarks and advertising.

## Information the app handles

- The reader downloads Sanskrit verses and Hindi explanations from a third-party open Gita dataset over HTTPS and keeps a local cache to reduce loading times.
- Saved verse IDs and daily notification settings are stored locally on the device. This prototype does not require user accounts or collect names/emails through its own forms.
- Internet connectivity state is checked to allow or block app access. No precise location permission is requested by the core reader.
- Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) integrations may collect or process information such as device identifiers, ad interactions, IP address, approximate location derived from IP and consent preferences, depending on configuration, region, device permissions and Google's policies. Consult [Google's privacy information](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google AdMob](https://admob.google.com/home/resources/).

## Advertising and consent

The app may display Google AdMob banner advertisements. If consent is required for your region, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present applicable choices. Availability and behavior depend on the app owner's AdMob account configuration. Production ad IDs must be configured before release.

## Notifications

Daily verse notifications are optional. You can disable them in the app Settings or Android notification controls. The scheduled time is approximate.

## Sharing

The app uses your device's standard share chooser only when you explicitly choose to share a verse. Data is then sent to the service that you select.

## Storage and retention

Downloaded Gita content, verse bookmarks and notification preferences reside on the device. They are ordinarily removed when the app is uninstalled. Advertising/consent data follows relevant Google platform policies.

## Third parties

- Dataset: Adiyogi Arts Bhagavad Gita JSON repository (MIT License), delivered via GitHub Raw or jsDelivr.
- Ads and consent management: Google AdMob and Google UMP.
